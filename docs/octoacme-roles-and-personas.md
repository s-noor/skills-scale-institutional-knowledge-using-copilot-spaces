# OctoAcme Project Management Roles & Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers lead user research, wireframing, and usability testing to ensure products are intuitive, accessible, and user-centered. They advocate for end-user needs throughout the product lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and create user personas / journey maps
- Design wireframes, prototypes, and user flows
- Lead usability testing and incorporate feedback into designs
- Collaborate with Product Manager on feature specifications
- Partner with Developers to ensure design feasibility and implementation fidelity
- Document design patterns and accessibility standards

### Goals
- Deliver products that delight users and meet their needs
- Reduce friction and improve user satisfaction
- Maintain consistency across the product experience
- Ensure accessibility and inclusive design principles

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with Developers and Product Manager
- User research findings and usability test results
- Design specifications and component documentation
- Feedback during sprint planning and retrospectives

### Interacts With
- Product Manager (feature scope and user validation)
- Developers (implementation and technical constraints)
- QA/Testing (accessibility and usability validation)
- Stakeholders (user feedback and organizational alignment)

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Facilitator

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master supports team delivery by facilitating Agile ceremonies, removing impediments, coaching the team on Agile values, and driving continuous improvement. They act as a servant leader focused on team health and process optimization.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, retrospectives, and backlog refinement
- Identify and escalate team impediments and blockers
- Coach team members on Agile practices and mindsets
- Maintain sprint boards and burndown charts
- Track team velocity and highlight process bottlenecks
- Support retrospectives and drive action item follow-up
- Mentor junior team members on delivery practices

### Goals
- Enable the team to deliver predictably and sustainably
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Remove organizational impediments that slow delivery
- Develop team maturity and self-organization

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation and coaching during ceremonies
- Escalation reports to Product Lead and Project Manager
- Metrics and insights on team health and velocity
- Action items and improvements from retrospectives

### Interacts With
- Developers (daily support, impediment removal)
- Project Manager (escalations, planning coordination)
- Product Manager (backlog readiness, refinement)
- Product Lead (organizational blockers and policy changes)
- QA/Testing (process alignment and DoD verification)

---

## Sponsor / Executive

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide strategic direction, funding, and escalation authority. They champion the project at leadership level, remove organizational barriers, and ensure alignment with business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Secure budget and resources for the project
- Communicate strategic vision and business context to the team
- Resolve escalated business and organizational risks
- Approve scope changes and major decisions
- Champion the project internally and remove executive-level blockers
- Ensure alignment with organizational strategy and priorities
- Participate in gate reviews and go/no-go decisions

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value and ROI
- Minimize organizational friction and political risk
- Maintain strategic alignment across the organization
- Enable team autonomy within business constraints

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based executive updates
- Escalation resolution and strategic guidance
- Go/no-go decision gates and approval sign-offs
- Retrospectives and lessons learned (post-project)

### Interacts With
- Product Lead (strategic alignment and trade-offs)
- Project Manager (escalations and resource decisions)
- Stakeholder groups (organizational coordination)
- Other Sponsors (cross-program dependencies)

---

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers ensure product and process documentation is structured, current, and accessible. They bridge communication between engineering teams and end users, reducing knowledge silos and supporting onboarding.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain user-facing product documentation
- Write and update release notes and migration guides
- Develop process and runbook documentation for team reference
- Support onboarding materials for new team members
- Collaborate with Developers to understand features and technical details
- Ensure documentation is clear, consistent, and accessible
- Maintain terminology glossaries and style guides

### Goals
- Reduce support burden by providing clear, self-service documentation
- Accelerate team onboarding and knowledge transfer
- Enable customers to adopt features and products quickly
- Minimize ambiguity and tribal knowledge dependencies

### Typical Communication
- Feature documentation and release notes
- Process guides and team runbooks
- Style guide updates and documentation standards
- Feedback from users and support teams on documentation gaps

### Interacts With
- Developers (understanding technical details and changes)
- Product Manager (feature context and user guides)
- Support / Customer Success (customer feedback and common questions)
- Project Manager (process documentation updates)
- QA/Testing (acceptance criteria and test scenarios)

---

## Stakeholder Groups

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Groups represent cross-functional teams and departments (e.g., Security, Legal, Sales, Support, Finance) who must be informed or consulted at key project phases. They ensure the project meets organizational standards and dependencies.

### Responsibilities
- Provide domain-specific requirements and constraints
- Review deliverables and gate decisions for compliance and impact
- Escalate concerns and dependencies early
- Participate in planning and design reviews when needed
- Sign off on critical decisions within their domain
- Share information about related initiatives and risks
- Support team with expertise and guidance

### Goals
- Ensure project meets organizational compliance and standards
- Reduce surprises and rework by catching issues early
- Maintain cross-functional alignment and reduce silos
- Protect organizational interests and manage risk

### Typical Communication
- Participation in planning and design reviews
- Requirements and constraint documentation
- Sign-offs at decision gates
- Risk and dependency updates in weekly syncs
- Post-release feedback and lessons learned

### Interacts With
- Project Manager (coordination and requirement gathering)
- Product Lead (trade-off decisions and approvals)
- Product Manager (feature scope and user impact)
- Developers (technical feasibility and implementation support)

### Examples of Stakeholder Groups
- **Security:** Ensure data protection, compliance, and threat mitigation
- **Legal / Compliance:** Validate contractual and regulatory requirements
- **Sales / Customer Success:** Ensure customer value and go-to-market readiness
- **Support / Operations:** Prepare for customer adoption and operational impact
- **Finance:** Track budget and resource allocation
- **Infrastructure / Platform:** Ensure scalability and platform compatibility

## How these personas are used in the exercise

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When running projects, map actual team members to these personas; some individuals may hold multiple roles in smaller teams.
- Update this guide as your organization evolves and discovers new patterns or role configurations.
